Skin By Deasys Corner

Client: Deasy

Project Type: Freelance Design Work

Tools Used: Canva / Adobe Photoshop

Duration: November 2025

Role: Graphic Designer

Project Overview

I collaborated with Skin By Deasys Corner (SKNBYDC), a skincare brand focused on enhancing natural beauty, to design a series of marketing flyers for their online store. The project began with a “Launching Soon” flyer to build anticipation for the brand's debut and will include future promotional materials highlighting products, offers, and campaigns. Each design was created to reflect the brand's calm, fresh, and radiant aesthetic combining soft tones, clean layouts, and minimal elegance to visually communicate trust, purity, and self-care.

My Role:

  • Graphic Designer
  • Responsible for conceptualizing and designing all flyer layouts for the brand’s campaigns.
  • Developed a cohesive visual direction that aligns with the brand’s skincare identity and online presence.
  • Created digital-ready flyers optimized for social media promotion and online marketing.
